Privacy Policy for Rangoli Arrows: Puzzle Game

Last Updated: October 7, 2026

Rangoli Arrows: Puzzle Game ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

Rangoli Arrows is an offline-first puzzle game. We do not require account registration, nor do we directly collect, store, or share personally identifiable user information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location).

2. Third-Party Services & Ads

Our app integrates third-party services that may process non-personally identifiable device identifiers for advertising and operational functionality:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used to display non-intrusive banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. AdMob may process non-personal data, such as Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, and general device telemetry to serve contextual and interest-based advertisements in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.
    • https://policies.google.com/privacy
    • https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

3. Advertising ID (AAID) Declaration

In compliance with Google Play Advertising ID policy (Android 13+), our app uses the Google Advertising ID solely for analytics, ad frequency capping, and serving personalized or contextual advertisements provided by Google Play Certified Ad Networks.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Our app is designed for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. All ads served through Google AdMob adhere to Google Play Families Policy guidelines.

5. Local Storage

Game progress, unlocked rangoli artwork, star scores, and coin balances are saved locally on your device using encrypted SharedPreferences. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app will erase your local game progress.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@vashudevgames.com.
